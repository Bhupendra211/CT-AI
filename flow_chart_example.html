📌 What is a Flowchart?

A flowchart is a diagram that shows steps of a process using shapes and arrows. It helps us understand how a problem is solved step by step.

🔷 Flowchart Symbols

  • Start: Beginning of process
  • Process: Action or step
  • Decision: Question (Yes/No)
  • End: Finish
